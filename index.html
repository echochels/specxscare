Corporate Strategy

How we plan to secure our competitive edge and achieve market leadership in the health-tech sector.

🏆 Winning Aspiration

To be the leading biomedical company in Nigeria by pioneering innovative digital healthcare solutions, leveraging cutting-edge biomedical technology and Artificial Intelligence.

🌍 Where the Business Plays

Specxs Care Limited focuses on addressing healthcare and health-tech challenges in Nigeria, particularly in the field of urinary incontinence, initially targeting the local market before expanding to Sub-Saharan Africa and Worldwide.

🎯 How We Win

By designing and manufacturing advanced medical devices and digital health solutions, such as the Smart Bladder Device, the CISRS snakebite response framework, and the Digital Kidney Wallet. We aim to revolutionize healthcare delivery, expand universal health coverage, and ensure affordability.

Business Architecture

Our Business Model

Our structural framework designed to deliver long-term value to patients, professionals, and healthcare partners.

Key Activities & Resources

  • Production of advanced medical devices, including the smart bladder device.
  • Continuous research and development in medical device design and technology.
  • Technical aid for healthcare professionals and end-users.
  • High-tech manufacturing facilities & knowledge capital.

Value Proposition & Markets

  • Affordable, non-invasive medical alternatives.
  • Integrity, innovation, and sustainable practices.
  • Target Segments: Healthcare facilities (hospitals, clinics) and professionals.
  • Partnerships with Government agencies and ministries of health.

Financially Sustainable Structure

  • Revenue: Direct product sales and licensing fees.
  • Revenue: Maintenance, installation, and clinical training service fees.
  • Cost: R&D cycles and clinical validation trials.
  • Cost: Regulatory certifications, setup, and manufacturing operations.
