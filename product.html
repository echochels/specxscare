Project 01

The Specxs Smart Bladder Device

A non-invasive, wireless electro-modulation wearable designed to restore normal bladder function and muscle coordination.

Specifications

Ergonomic & Wireless Design

Engineered for discreet wear, comfort, and clinical effectiveness under everyday conditions.

Application Areas

The device is designed for easy abdominal or pelvic application using comfortable, adjustable elastic straps.

Pelvic / Abdominal Placement
01

Ultra-Light & Ergonomic

Weighing only 1.8 grams, the device features a compact frame measuring 5 cm by 3.8 cm, designed for comfortable, long-term discreet wear.

02

100% Wireless Technology

No visible external wires or skin-penetrating electrodes. Low-level electrical impulses stimulate the nerve pathway non-invasively.

03

Adjustable Compression Straps

Features adjustable, hypoallergenic straps to keep the stimulation module firmly in place during physical activity or rest.

Clinical Applications

Types of Incontinence Treated

Restoring pelvic health and muscle co-ordination for a wide range of clinical diagnoses.

Stress Incontinence

Supports bladder control and prevents leaks during physical activities, laughing, coughing, or stress. Validated primarily in adult populations.

Urgency Incontinence

Effectively manages sudden, intense urges to urinate by calming hyper-reactive bladder nerves via gentle electrical stimulation.

Overflow Incontinence

Aids in nerve re-sensitization to prevent passive leaking from an overfilled or under-active bladder system.

Urinary Retention

Promotes and stimulates contractions in the bladder wall muscles, helping patients who struggle to fully empty their bladder.

Complete Incontinence (VVF)

Indicated for severe conditions, including Vesico-Vaginal Fistula (VVF), depending on clinical severity and doctor recommendations.

Clinical Trial Results

Evidence-based results demonstrating the therapeutic efficacy of Specxs electro-stimulation.

Bladder sensitivity increase (Adult Males) 62%

Key Findings & Patient Outcomes

Clinical trials confirm that the Specxs Smart Bladder Device triggers a substantial reduction in leakage events while boosting patient confidence and overall quality of life.

Failures or non-responses were rare. Minimal improvements were mostly documented in patients suffering from advanced secondary neuropathy caused by diabetes, stroke, or chronic hypertension.

⚠ Safety & Skin Sensitization Note

About 20% of volunteers reported mild skin rashes or itching at the contact point. Subsequent clinical evaluations confirmed that skin sensitivity thresholds differ; some patients require lower impulse thresholds, which can be adjusted on the device.

Project 02: Public Health Integration

Community-Integrated Snakebite Response System (CISRS)

Saving lives, bridging communities, and strengthening health systems through cultural integration and digital tracking.

The Community-Integrated Snakebite Response System (CISRS) introduces a novel, community-driven approach to snakebite management by formally integrating traditional healers into the healthcare system. Rather than bypassing existing cultural practices, it leverages them to improve early response, safety, and hospital referrals.

Three Key Pillars of Innovation:

01

Integration Model (Traditional Healer Training)

Training and certifying traditional healers as first responders. Rather than competing, this bridges the gap between informal community practices and formal medical facilities, ensuring a "do no harm" approach and immediate patient stabilization.

02

Decentralized Treatment (PHC Upgrades)

Expanding access to quality, temperature-controlled antivenom directly to Primary Healthcare Centres (PHCs). This minimizes treatment delays and reduces the dependency on distant, over-burdened referral hospitals.

03

Digital Tracking & Surveillance

Deploying simple, low-cost mobile tracking tools to enable real-time reporting of bites, envenomation severity, and antivenom inventory. This improves surveillance, resource mapping, and response efficiency in low-resource settings.

A Highly Adaptable Model: This culturally sensitive, cost-effective approach is highly adaptable to similar public health contexts across Africa and other low-resource regions facing high-risk snakebite emergencies.

Project 03: AI Diagnostics & Sensing

Digital Kidney Wallet

Specxs Digital Kidney Wallet Wearable and Diagnostic Sensor System

The Digital Kidney Wallet is an innovative, low-cost digital health solution designed to strengthen kidney disease prevention, early detection, and long-term management. Leveraging mobile technology, the platform securely stores kidney screening results, generates personalized health reminders, and provides lifestyle guidance to support continuous patient engagement and care.

Each user is assigned a secure digital health record that can be accessed by both patients and healthcare providers, helping to bridge critical gaps in kidney health monitoring and follow-up care.

Beyond traditional screening, the Digital Kidney Wallet incorporates a sensor-enabled diagnostic system capable of detecting heavy metals associated with kidney damage, combined with an Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered risk assessment platform. The system identifies individuals at elevated risk of developing kidney disease and provides predictive insights based on health indicators and lifestyle patterns. This enables early intervention, personalized prevention strategies, and proactive disease management before irreversible kidney damage occurs.
